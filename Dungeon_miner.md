# **⛏️ Dungeon Miner**

## **🕹️ Level 1: Title & Hook**

**Game Name:** Dungeon Miner

I chose the name Dungeon Miner because you are basically a miner who goes into a giant dungeon and mines stuff. The deeper you go, the better stuff you can find, but there are also more monsters. The goal is to get as far down as possible and figure out what is at the bottom.

---

## **🕹️ Level 2: Genre**

**Core Genre:** Adventure, Action

**Scrolling / Perspective:** Vertical scrolling or multi-directional

**Movement & Abilities:** fighting, emoting, power/spells that do damage

**Action / Conflict:** Combat, survival, melee attacks, spells boss fights

**Structure / Progression:** Level-based, gold based

**Aesthetic / Tone:** Pixel art, cartoonish

**Challenge Style:** Exploration, resource management, strategy

Dungeon Miner is a 2D game where you explore a huge underground dungeon. You mine blocks to get materials and gold, find treasure, and fight monsters. You start with basic equipment and get better equipment as you go deeper with your gold. There are different areas with stronger enemies and better resources. Some paths are safer while others have better rewards. At the end of major areas, there are bosses that you have to defeat to continue.

---

## **🕹️ Level 3: Backstory**

The game takes place underneath a small town that has a giant abandoned mine next to it. Nobody knows how deep the mine goes because everyone who tried to explore it eventually gave up. One day, the main character decides to go into the mine because they heard there was a bunch of valuable treasure underground. The mine starts out normal with dirt, stone, and a few annoying bats. However, the deeper the character goes, the stranger everything gets. There are giant caves, lava areas, old buildings, and monsters that definitely should not be living underground. There are also random signs and drawings from people who explored the mine a long time ago.

The main character eventually finds out that the mine is actually part of an ancient underground kingdom. A long time ago, the kingdom was destroyed by a giant monster called the Dungeon King. The survivors sealed the monster at the bottom of the mine and left the area. The main character accidentally starts breaking the seals while mining because they are looking for better materials. Now the Dungeon King is slowly waking up. The character has to keep going deeper to find the treasure and stop the monster before it gets out. Basically, the character caused the problem because they wanted some shiny rocks.

---

## **🕹️ Level 4: Characters**

### **Protagonist**

The main character is a normal miner who wears a helmet, boots, and a backpack. They start with a bad pickaxe and a small sword. They are mostly motivated by finding treasure and becoming stronger. They also want to see what is at the bottom of the mine because nobody else has made it that far.

### **Antagonists**

The enemies are underground monsters like bats, skeletons, slimes, spiders, and giant rock monsters. They get stronger as the player goes deeper. The main villain is the Dungeon King, who lives at the very bottom of the dungeon.

### **Other Key Characters**

There is an old miner who lives near the entrance. He gives the player random advice, although some of it is not very useful. He also sells equipment and tells the player stories about the mine.

---

## **🕹️ Level 5: Target Market / Audience**

The game would be for people around **12 and older** who like Minecraft, and other adventure games. Players who like mining, collecting items, fighting enemies, and exploring would probably enjoy it. The pixel art would also make the game feel simple and fun instead of super serious. Mostly like grinding games with bragging rights.

---

## **🕹️ Level 6: Player Motivations**

Players keep playing because they want to get better items and explore deeper into the dungeon to get more rich and to get stronger. Finding rare materials lets the player upgrade their weapons, armor, and pickaxe. There are also hidden rooms with treasure and secret areas. Players will want to see what is at the bottom and eventually fight the Dungeon King.

---

## **🕹️ Level 7: Unique Selling Point (USP)**

The main thing that makes Dungeon Miner different is that **the deeper you go, the crazier the dungeon gets**. The player starts in a normal mine but eventually finds giant caves, lava areas, ancient ruins, and weird monsters. The player also has to manage their resources because they need materials for weapons, armor, and tools. And maybe it could have clans where you team up with your friends to make the game easier or have lobbies to join others.

Another feature is that mining can sometimes reveal secret areas. 

---

## **🕹️ Level 8: Inspirations & Competition**

### **Inspirations**

**Minecraft:** I would take the mining, crafting, and exploration ideas from Minecraft. I would make my game 2D and more focused on fighting and completing levels.

**Terraria:** I would take inspiration from Terraria's 2D exploration, enemies, items, and bosses. My game would be more focused on going deeper instead of building a giant house.

### **Competition**

The main game Dungeon Miner would compete with is **Terraria** because both games involve 2D exploration, mining, fighting monsters, and collecting resources. Dungeon Miner would be different because it would have a more straightforward story and focus on going deeper into one giant dungeon and getting rich.

---

## **🕹️ Level 9: Goals**

I want players to feel like they are actually exploring somewhere they have never been before. The game should be fun and a little challenging without being insanely hard. I also want players to get excited when they find rare items or secret rooms. The bosses should be difficult enough that beating them feels rewarding. Overall, the game is supposed to be a fun adventure where you mine rocks, fight random monsters, get better stuff, and eventually fight a giant underground guy because you probably shouldn't have been mining there in the first place.
